# Sound credits

- `fan_normal.ogg`: 55 s cut from "WWS Ventilationfan.ogg" by Work With Sounds, CC BY 4.0,
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Ventilationfan.ogg (16 kHz mono, spin-up and spin-down removed).
  The broken fan sounds are MicroAware's simulated faults (`microaware/synth.py`) mixed into it.
- `mimii/*.ogg`: MIMII dataset, Purohit et al., "MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine
  Investigation and Inspection", Hitachi, 2019, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://zenodo.org/records/3384388.
  id_00 at 6 dB, file 00000000 of normal and abnormal, channel 0. Fetched with `tools/fetch_mimii.py`.
